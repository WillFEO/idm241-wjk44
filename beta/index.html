Microinteractions Beta: The Pinterest "Pin" Card

Objects: A single pin card in the Pinterest home feed/search results grid (logged-in user), its board selector (the "Profile" button in the top left), and its "Save" button.

Scope: Hovering on and off of the pin card, its board selector, and its "Save" button (both unsaved and saved), and clicking "Save" to save and unsave the pin. The Share and "..." buttons are not included in this scope.

The Link

https://www.pinterest.com/

This link will open in a new tab. You will need a free account to view the content and microinteractions.

Locating the Object

Once you are logged in, move your mouse onto any unsaved pin and click "Save". The current version has no transitions, and its save confirmation appears at the bottom of the screen.

The Microinteraction Breakdown

Triggers

Pin Card

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF

Board Selector (the "Profile" button)

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF

"Save" Button (Unsaved)

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF
  • Toggle ON Click

"Save" Button (Saved)

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF
  • Toggle OFF Click

Rules

Pin Card

  • Hover ON
    • The default arrow cursor instantly swaps to the pointing-finger hand
    • A vertical gradient overlay fades in over 150ms, 40% opacity at the top (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)) and fading to 5% at the bottom (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05))
    • Three buttons fade in with the overlay: a board selector in the top left (#FFFFFF white text and chevron on a 30% black background, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)), a "Save" button with #FFFFFF white text in the top right (#E60023 red, or #333333 dark grey reading "Saved" if the pin is already saved), and a #FFFFFF white Share button with a #111111 black icon in the bottom right
  • Hover OFF
    • The pointing-finger hand cursor instantly swaps back to the default arrow
    • The vertical gradient overlay fades out over 150ms
    • The buttons fade out with the overlay, returning the pin card to its original state

Board Selector (the "Profile" button)

  • Hover ON
    • The background darkens to 50% black (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)) over 150ms
  • Hover OFF
    • The background fades back to 30% black (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)) over 150ms

"Save" Button (Unsaved)

  • Hover ON
    • The button darkens from #E60023 red to #AD081B red over 150ms
  • Hover OFF
    • The button fades back to #E60023 red over 150ms
  • Toggle ON Click
    • The button shrinks to 95% over 100ms while pressed and springs back over 150ms on release
    • The text springs into "Saved!" over 800ms: it pops up to 115% size in the first 160ms, then settles back to 100% over the next 300ms with a slight wobble
    • Six short #FFFFFF white lines burst out from the button and fade away over 950ms
    • The button fades from #AD081B red to #111111 near-black (the Saved hover color, since the cursor is still on it) and widens to fit the text over 150ms, staying anchored to the top right corner
    • After 1 second, "Saved!" fades into "Saved" over 150ms, and the button narrows to fit the text over 150ms
    • Backend: the pin would be added to the board named in the board selector ("Profile") in the database

"Save" Button (Saved)

  • Hover ON
    • The button darkens from #333333 dark grey to #111111 near-black over 150ms
  • Hover OFF
    • The button fades back to #333333 dark grey over 150ms
  • Toggle OFF Click
    • The button shrinks to 95% over 100ms while pressed and springs back over 150ms on release
    • The text fades back to "Save" and the button narrows to fit over 150ms
    • The button fades from #111111 near-black to #AD081B red (the Unsaved hover color, since the cursor is still on it) over 150ms
    • Backend: the pin would be removed from that board in the database

The Share button and the "..." button below the card are not part of this microinteraction. Share fades in and out with the overlay, and "..." stays visible and unchanged in all states.

The backend updates are hypothetical, since this redesign is a concept and is not connected to Pinterest.
